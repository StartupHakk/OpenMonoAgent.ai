# Shorts script — OpenMono / StartupHakk branded

**Working title:** 32 agents run my ads. I just approve.

**Length:** ~45–60 seconds

## Hook
So this is what it looks like when **OpenMono agents** run your entire ad operation — and it can replace a marketing agency.

## Promise
They handle the research, the creatives, the launches, the budgets, and the reports.
It never spends more than you set.
And **no ad goes live until you sign off**.

## Module 1 — Research & creative
Claude studies your competitors’ ads, your best reviews, and what your customers keep saying — then writes the angles.
Image and video agents turn them into ads. ElevenLabs adds the voiceover.
So you test **ten new ads a week** instead of one — and every ad gives a real reason to buy.

## Module 2 — Launch & optimize
Once you approve, it launches on Meta, TikTok, and Google with the right audience and a small test budget.
Every few hours it checks the numbers and pauses anything wasting money.
So no ad burns your budget for days — and the winners show up fast.

## Module 3 — Profit tracking
It tracks which ads brought in **real sales**, not just clicks — and what each customer actually cost you.
Then it proposes moving budget to the winners.
So every dollar goes where it makes you money — and you always know your real return.

## Control plane
And running the whole thing: **OpenMono + Claude Code** — connected to your ad accounts, your store, and your CRM.
Every morning you get one page: what’s sold, what you spent, and where to move the budget.

## Close
AI tests the ads. **You** pick the winners.
Comment **ADS** and I’ll send you the StartupHakk blueprint.
